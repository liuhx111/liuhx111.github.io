Robot Autonomy and Intelligence Lab (RAIL)

The Robot Autonomy and Intelligence Lab (RAIL), led by Hangxin Liu at the School of Intelligence Science and Technology, Peking University, studies cognitive robotics and embodied intelligence. We aim to develop robots that can understand their surroundings, reason about the physical and functional properties of the world, and carry out complex tasks autonomously.

Our research spans robot perception and scene understanding, physical reasoning, task and motion planning, robot learning, and cross-embodiment skill transfer. We are particularly interested in closing the loop between perception, reasoning, action, and learning so that robots can adapt to diverse environments and collaborate naturally with people.
